# Versioning Policy

## Semantic Versioning
- MAJOR: incompatible behavior changes.
- MINOR: new skills, agents, templates, or capabilities.
- PATCH: wording fixes and non-breaking clarity improvements.

## Current Baseline
- Ecosystem: 1.0.0

## Update Rules
1. Update MANIFEST.md for every new or changed asset.
2. Append release notes to CHANGELOG.md.
3. Re-run validation scenarios after MINOR or MAJOR changes.
